# v1.4.7 — Settle accepted chat drafts and recover missing sync objects

**Release track:** release

**Date:** 2026-09-12

## Summary

v1.4.7 fixes chat drafts stranded while checking durable acceptance, adds bounded recovery of missing sync objects, and makes MCP startup independent of service initialization with connection status based on confirmed provider evidence.

## Highlights

- Settle accepted composer submissions across sends and reconnects while preserving a newer draft.
- Recover verified missing sync objects through one owned operation per realm, with durable retry receipts and automatic startup verification.
- Register MCP tools without initializing the service store, and report late provider startup failures accurately.

## Fixed

- The composer reconciles durable prompt receipts even when live delivery or reconnect races the submission response. Accepted submissions settle without clearing a newer draft or leaving the composer stuck checking acceptance.
- Missing-object recovery validates canonical references and authenticated peer objects before repair, without changing durable refs. Caller timeouts and cancellation leave the owned operation running; retries retain their exact operation identity and truthful result.
- Startup verification resumes interrupted recovery with bounded attempt history, preserves earlier failures, and updates readiness only after checking current durable and projection heads. Stale-head requests and unrelated realm failures remain fenced.
- Ordinary and assigned MCP entrypoints register the existing proxy tools without booting service lifecycles, opening SQLite, or writing service state. Assigned bindings retain their exact capability checks.
- MCP health stays pending until explicit startup success or a fresh successful PA tool invocation confirms the current provider session. Late failures update live status; historical, stale-session, or pre-failure completions cannot falsely restore connectivity. Existing timeouts remain unchanged.
