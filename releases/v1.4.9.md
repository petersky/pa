# v1.4.9 — Clear operation status and accountable fleet health

**Release track:** release

**Date:** 2026-09-12

## Summary

v1.4.9 makes PA's operation status, instance scope diagnostics, completion requirements, and operational problem reporting more explicit. Reading status remains passive; recovery is a separate action. Declared acceptance governs completion and cleanup, while an explicitly configured authority can gather fleet problems and coordinate one repair workflow.

## Changed

- Operation status reads bounded owner receipts without scheduling reconciliation or repeating an effect. Explicit authenticated recovery preserves the operation's identity and coalesces repeated admissions. Missing or unavailable evidence remains pending or unavailable, never proof of success. Independently authenticated local operational routes remain usable during canonical-history recovery; canonical writes retain their dependency and realm gates.
- GitHub supervision distinguishes local saved scope from each instance's advertised scope, with precise causes for stale capabilities, authority outages, invalid configuration, rejected credentials, unavailable verification, repository access failures, and explicit scope denial. Historical advertisements are diagnostic evidence only. Scope changes still require validation and confirmation of the exact list on the owning instance.
- Cards can declare completion and verification requirements. A merged PR or completed worker turn cannot satisfy an outstanding deployment or acceptance milestone, and workspace cleanup must respect those requirements. Acceptance is tied to current canonical evidence and the declared eligible owner; missing ownership or verification keeps protected work waiting.
- The durable operational problem journal records immutable observations and revisions, tracks collection custody separately from resolution, and preserves links to repair cards, commits, PRs, and acceptance. One explicitly configured fleet authority gathers reports with bounded retries and coordinates one nonterminal repair action through PA's normal dispatch and PR supervision workflow.
- The chat composer stays visible while messages are queued.

## Compatibility and limits

- Environment-token-only installations without a saved supervision scope no longer receive an implicit unrestricted grant. Establish an explicit repository list through Settings' normal preview and exact-scope confirmation. Existing valid explicit and legacy scopes retain their documented semantics; invalid or unavailable policy fails closed without changing permissions.
- Health collection and scheduling are disabled on new instances until an administrator configures consistent fleet authority and enables it. There is no automatic authority election or partition failover. Unavailable peers remain unverified; gathered reports are not resolved reports.
- Health repairs require declared canonical protection before repair admission and independent, authenticated acceptance covering the actual scenario and affected instances. The originating repair session cannot certify its own deployment. Pause does not cancel effects already admitted. Authority transfer is explicitly fenced and bounded; unresolved obligations or oversized transfers block it.
- This release does not itself configure fleet policy, deploy repairs, or establish production acceptance. Publication, installation, activation, and verification retain their normal explicit authority requirements. A source merge or package smoke test is not evidence that the fleet has been upgraded or verified.

## Upgrade

After publication, use `pa update` through the normal authorized installation workflow. Review instance scope and health authority configuration separately; upgrading does not enable the collector.
