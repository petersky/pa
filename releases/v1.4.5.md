# v1.4.5 — Reliable continuations and live history recovery

**Release track:** release

**Date:** 2026-09-11

## Summary

v1.4.5 repairs pending notification continuation routing, makes dispatch prompt admission and retries recoverable with a durable identity, and clears stale history-retry warnings after live chat catches up.

## Highlights

- Recover an eligible pending MCP notification continuation into an explicitly verified successor while preserving the original question, choices, response, and audit history.
- Retry initial and follow-up dispatch delivery using the same durable prompt identity, including after lost acknowledgements and interrupted admission.
- Clear recovered live-history warnings without hiding independent paging errors.

## Fixed

- Notification continuation transfer requires the originating operator identity and matching execution provenance. Invalid or shared fleet credentials cannot authorize a transfer; ambiguous or provider-owned continuations remain ineligible. Transfer never supplies consent or automatically sends a failed response.
- Notification response stages preserve concurrent read and acknowledgement metadata, and continuation links point to the verified successor. Retries acknowledge an already admitted response even after that successor closes, without enqueueing it again.
- Initial and follow-up dispatches retain exact prompt acceptance and outcome receipts across redaction, concurrent retries, lost acknowledgements, and queue/checkpoint recovery. Provider execution stays fenced until durable admission completes, and the initial authority receipt survives local target acknowledgement.
- Dispatch delivery checks protocol compatibility before sending to an older target. Ambiguous legacy operations without a durable identity are not automatically replayed.
- Successful live gap recovery retires its retry banner after concurrent SSE catch-up; unrelated history paging failures remain visible and retryable.
