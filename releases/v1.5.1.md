# v1.5.1 — 1.5.1

**Release track:** release  
**Date:** 2026-09-23

## Summary

Patch release focused on Home lifecycle accuracy, attachment admission durability, and chat recovery reliability. Operators and users who hit stalled sessions, misleading Home cards, or rejected verified attachments after interrupt/restart should upgrade.

## Highlights

- Home no longer treats settled or completed work as live motion, and failed attempts offer Inspect instead of misleading retry advice
- Verified attachment evidence survives admission replay and ledger reload instead of being discarded on same-instance dispatch
- Agent teardown race fixed so restarts recover after a child exits during ACP shutdown
- Queue Resume controls refresh live when interrupts pause pending prompts
- Session recovery banners show failure-specific guidance instead of generic project-availability advice

## Changed

- Shared Home/Work/session presentation respects explicit pauses, ignores settled recovery metadata, and keeps unscheduled completion records visible for inspection without claiming motion
- Ended turns on unfinished Active/Waiting cards surface as Work unfinished, including legacy cards without a completion requirement
- Recovery banners and empty-chat status use the current recovery remedy or error; workspace guidance is retained only for blocked provisioning

## Fixed

- Home lifecycle evidence could mark completed cards as active, recommend retrying historical failures, or label an ended turn as task completion
- Same-instance attachment dispatch could drop freshly verified evidence when an admission record already existed
- Provider exit between ACP shutdown timeout and terminate raised `ProcessLookupError` and aborted restart; failed quiescence could leave saved prompts queued indefinitely
- Browser queue controls did not subscribe to `publication_fence_established`, so Resume stayed unavailable after an interrupt until reload
- Blocked sessions showed Correct the project availability even when the real failure was lost provider context or another recovery error
