## Summary

PA handles routine card verification with independent agents, keeps completion
evidence exact, and makes conversation navigation clearer.

## Highlights

- Routine repair verification uses normal agent dispatch and session lifecycle
  checks, preserving explicit human decisions and permission settings.
- Verification shares the existing operational journal and service loop.
- A transient GitHub mergeability lookup no longer loses genuine stable-green
  proof. Original merged watches can reconcile their audit evidence safely.
- Selected conversation issues have their own completion scope, and their
  receipts open the correct card. Finished clean runs no longer block the first
  admission after reopening a card.
- Conversation history and the Chats heading include the already merged fixes.
