# v1.5.3 — 1.5.3

**Release track:** release  
**Date:** 2026-10-15

## Summary

This release adds independent conversation branches, browser sign-in with account navigation, and smoother chat history. Older messages now load as you scroll up, and recent history comes back without replaying the whole conversation. Work admission is more durable, queue cues are more accurate, and evidence handling for review and supervision is more reliable. Anyone who uses the web chat, discussion work queues or supervisor review flows should upgrade.

## Highlights

- Conversations can split into independent branches that share next actors.
- Browser sign-in, with navigation for signed-in accounts that fits different screen sizes.
- Older conversation history loads when you scroll up, without moving your place in the conversation.
- Discussion issue lists and durable start-selected work admission, with guidance for chat agents.
- The queue now shows its true order, whether items are eligible, and where they came from.

## Added

- Independent conversation branches and shared next actors.
- Browser sign-in and navigation for signed-in accounts.
- Bounded card discovery and conversation timing diagnostics.
- Discussion issue lists and durable start-selected work admission.
- Conversation titles from the provider, a Terminal evidence viewer, and direct chat entry.
- Older conversation history loads when you scroll up, without moving anchors.
- Read-only merge evidence is checkpointed for exact-head review.

## Changed

- Effort choices are limited to the selected model and native route.
- The queue shows its true order, whether items are eligible, and their source.
- Chat agents are guided through durable selected work admission.
- The browser evidence variable now lives outside the application configuration namespace.

## Fixed

- Recent chat history recovers without replaying the conversation from the beginning.
- Supervisor evidence lookup stays stable when the executor retries.
- New chat stays reachable next to the responsive account navigation.
- Compatibility with older presentation and the conversation tool contracts is preserved.

pa update
