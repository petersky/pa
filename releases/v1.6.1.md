# v1.6.1

**Release track:** release  
**Date:** 2026-11-08

## Summary

Operators can now dismiss a failed restart handoff, which previously blocked closing its session, selected workspace cleanup and PR-supervisor card completion indefinitely. This release also carries the selected-lease workspace cleanup tooling, durable scheduling with recipient-owned delivery, and workspace-recovery hardening merged since v1.6.0. Anyone with a session stuck on `restart_continuation_pending` should upgrade.

## Highlights

- New `dismissed` restart-handoff status and `POST /api/agent/sessions/{id}/restart-handoffs/{hid}/dismiss` (#656).
- Selected cleanup of exact fenced workspace leases (`workspace_collect_selected`), with untracked-file preservation and final-head comparison (#651-#655).
- Durable scheduling and recipient-owned delivery, including overdue-delivery visibility (#648-#650).
- Recovery of exact empty-index workspaces without discarding matching files (#645).

## Added

- Dismiss a failed restart handoff. It is allowed only from `failed`, never restarts PA or sends a prompt, keeps the receipt, prompt and history for audit, claims no effect either way, refuses in-flight handoffs, is idempotent, and cannot be retried afterwards. Dismissed handoffs are excluded from the pending-continuation check, the competing-handoff checks, startup recovery scans and the restart-preview tool.
- Selected workspace cleanup by explicit lease, fence, HEAD and timestamp, with GitHub merge revalidation as cleanup-only evidence.
- Durable scheduling with recipient-owned delivery.
- Bounded native session-restoration diagnostics.

## Changed

- Failed restart handoffs are surfaced without replaying their effects (#642).
- Existing sessions keep their authority selection receipts when resumed (#643).
- Workspace repair rejection reasons are exposed on an allowlist (#646).

## Fixed

- A failed restart handoff no longer blocks session close, cleanup or card completion forever once dismissed.
- Missed once-only schedules honor the skip policy; JSON payloads and scheduling controls paginate correctly.
- Workspace recovery retains matching files during index repair and isolates preserved recovery until worker completion.

## Known issues

- Handoffs that failed before this release stay `failed` until dismissed through the new endpoint; nothing dismisses them automatically.
- The paged cleanup-provenance feature is parked (reverted in #651); its source remains at `11a5c7d8`.

## Upgrade

```bash
pa update
# or fresh install:
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/petersky/pa/main/scripts/install-remote.sh | bash
```
